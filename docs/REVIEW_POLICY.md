# Google reviews: the rules, and what the team can do instead

*Summary for staff. Google and consumer-protection rules change, so check Google's current "Prohibited and restricted content" and
review-policy pages, and your local law, before relying on this. It is not legal advice.*

## The rules that matter
1. **No review gating.** You may not ask only happy customers to review on Google and send unhappy ones somewhere private.
   Sending 3-5 stars to Google and 1-2 stars to a private form is gating. Risks: reviews removed, a Business Profile warning or
   suspension, and legal exposure (for example the US FTC's rule against suppressing negative reviews, and similar rules in the
   UK, EU and India). **This app deliberately shows the Google link to every rating.**
2. **No incentives for reviews** (discounts, gifts, entries) and no offers to change or remove a review in exchange for anything.
3. **No fake or conflict-of-interest reviews**: staff, family or friends reviewing, or anyone buying reviews.
4. **Private feedback is allowed** as long as it is offered *in addition to* the public review, never *instead of* it.

## Can we stop someone posting a fake or negative review directly on Google?
No. Anyone with a Google account can review the profile without using our QR code. Only Google can remove a review.
What we control is how fast we respond and how many genuine reviews we have.

## What the team does (the workaround that is allowed)
| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Genuine unhappy customer | Reply publicly within 1-2 days: apologise, no excuses, invite them to contact the salon. Fix the problem. If they choose to update their review, that is their decision. |
| Review looks fake (never visited, wrong business, spam, competitor, abuse, off-topic) | Log it under Admin > **Google reviews**, write the reason, assign an owner, report it on Google (Reviews > ⋮ > Report review), and check back in a few days. Appeal through Google's review-removal request form if it stays. |
| Google keeps it | Mark it *Kept*, reply calmly and factually. A measured reply helps more than the review hurts. |
| Dozens of fake reviews / harassment campaign | Gather screenshots and dates, contact Google Business Profile support, and consider legal advice. |

## What helps long-term (and SEO)
- Ask **every** happy customer, at the point of service, with the QR code on the counter, receipt and WhatsApp follow-up.
  A steady flow of real reviews dilutes outliers and improves local ranking.
- Customers describe real services and staff in their own words. The AI only rephrases what they wrote; do not add keywords for them.
- Reply to every review (positive too). Responses are visible to searchers.

## How the app supports this
- `/review`: every rating gets the same Google link. Ratings of 1-3 additionally see a prominent "Tell the salon privately" card.
- **Admin > Feedback**: private feedback inbox (status, notes, CSV export).
- **Admin > Google reviews**: log reviews posted on Google, assign an owner, draft replies from templates, flag/report suspected fakes
  (a reason is required), and track *Reported / Removed / Kept*. Entries are logged manually; the app cannot read, hide or delete
  Google reviews (that would need Google Business Profile API access).
